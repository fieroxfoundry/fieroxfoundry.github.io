About me

Hoi!

I'm Edwin, the creator behind Fierox Foundry (known as Fierox88 online). What began as a hobby and personal collecting evolved into a full-fledged forge of creation. Fueled by my love for 3D modeling and sculpting, I use both resin and FDM 3D printing to cast my digital designs into physical reality.

Inside this foundry, you'll find creations heavily inspired by Sci-Fi, Fantasy, Mythology, the Dark Arts, Movies, and Gaming. Everything from fanart to more technical and darker works.

My little workshop is located in The Netherlands, where I live with my two cats, Cosmo & Nova.

Enjoy your time in the forge! If you have any questions about my work or would like to know more, feel free to send me a message via Instagram or e-mail.
