Amnio Tank PC

An RDA Labs (Avatar) inspired case for my BC-250. A former crypto mining machine turned budget Steam Machine. It has a Na'vi BJD inside an Amnio tank and some Pandora/alien looking airplants to finish the RDA labs look. The 3D printed case for the BC-250 is a case made by Kacikor on Printables heavily modded by me to support the powersupply, airplants and Amnio Tank (lightning). Two Arctic P14Max fans and a heatsink on the backplate keep the BC-250 cool. I changed the size, changed it to fit my PSU and added the plants plus other small changes. The Amnio tank, the little Turzx screen and the plants are connected and removable using magnets.
